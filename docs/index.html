How to use

  1. Type an English word or phrase in the box.
  2. Tap Show pronunciation (or press Enter). You will see the phonetic transcription in IPA and hear it read aloud.
  3. Tap ▶ to listen again. Turn on Slow to hear it at a slower speed.
  4. Stress marks: ˈ comes before the syllable with main stress, ˌ before secondary stress. A long vowel is shown with ː.
  5. Single words are shown in their full (dictionary) form. In fast speech, short words like to, can or are are often reduced to /tə/, /kən/, /ə/.

No sound? Check that your phone is not on silent and turn up the media volume.
